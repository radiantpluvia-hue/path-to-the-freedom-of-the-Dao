# Complete List of Races, Summaries, and Passives

Below are concise, improved summaries for each race along with 2–4 passive traits you can use in design or implement directly in-game. Background lists are preserved for reference where useful. Percent chance values reflect adjusted spawn/selection weights supplied by the designer.

## 1. Human (68% — versatile, adaptable)
Humans are the backbone of mortal realms. They rely on ingenuity, training, and social bonds rather than innate supernatural advantage. Humans progress steadily, excel at learning diverse techniques, and adapt to circumstances through effort and relationships.

Suggested passives (pick 1–2):
- Tenacious Learner: +8% cultivation/XP gain from training and practice.
- Socially Resourceful: +10% effectiveness on social checks (barter, persuasion, recruitment) and slightly improved loot value from merchants.

Backgrounds (for reference):
- **Noble Family**, **Common Folk**, **Street Orphan**, **Scholar Family**, **Merchant Family**, **Artisan Lineage**, **Mountain Seclusion**

## 2. Demon (7% — fierce, willful)
Demons harness raw will and conflict to transform power. Their cultivation is aggressive and direct; they excel in combat, intimidation, and manipulating darker energies.

Suggested passives (3–4):
- Martial Vigor: +10% physical damage while below 50% health.
- Predatory Insight: +8% critical strike chance against single or isolated targets.
- Fiendish Resolve: After defeating an enemy, gain a short-duration damage buff.
- Forbidden Alchemy: Small chance to generate rare reagents from combat encounters.

Backgrounds (for reference):
- **Demon Royal Bloodline**, **Demon Warrior Clan**, **Demon Outcast**, **Demon Sage Lineage**, **Demon Assassin Guild**, **Demon Alchemist**, **Demon Beast Tamer**

## 3. Spirit (8% — ephemeral, attuned)
Spirits are embodiments of domains and forces. They excel at subtle manipulation of the environment, elemental resonance, and utility—trading direct brute power for control, speed, or mystical effects.

Suggested passives (3–4):
- Elemental Affinity: +12% potency with a chosen element and reduced Qi cost for elemental techniques.
- Insubstantial Presence: -10% incoming physical damage and partial immunity to grapples or immobilizing effects.
- Domain Echo: Small chance to trigger a domain-related environmental effect (minor heal, spark, gust) on ability use.
- Lucid Memory: Reduced training time for esoteric or mental techniques.

Backgrounds (for reference):
- **Ancient Spirit**, **Elemental Spirit**, **Nature Spirit**, **Wandering Spirit**, **Celestial Spirit**, **Chaos Spirit**, **Dream Spirit**

## 4. Dragon (0.9% — majestic, overpowering)
Dragons embody scale, longevity, and elemental sovereignty. They are rare and often dominate localized ecosystems through sheer presence. Dragons combine natural tankiness with devastating single-target abilities and domain control.

Suggested passives (3–4):
- Dragonhide: +20% damage reduction against physical attacks.
- Sovereign Aura: Allies within range gain +6% damage or defense.
	- Elemental Dominion: Access to a lineage elemental burst (storm/earth/emptiness) with a moderate cooldown.
- Ancient Resilience: Slow growth curve but strong stat boosts at key thresholds.

Backgrounds (for reference):
	- **Imperial Dragon Bloodline**, **Ancient Dragon Clan**, **Young Dragon**, **Exiled Dragon**, **Storm Dragon Heritage**, **Earth Dragon Lineage**, **Emptiness Dragon Bloodline**

## 5. Phoenix (0.9% — cyclical, purifying)
Phoenixes are avatars of rebirth and purification: potent at burst damage and recovery mechanics that cleanse or reset harmful states.

Suggested passives (3–4):
- Rebirth Ember: Rare chance to avoid fatal damage and instead gain a temporary invulnerability shield.
- Purifying Flames: Attacks remove one minor debuff on hit or reduce corruption.
- Thermic Surge: Periodic area-of-effect bursts tied to resource thresholds.
- Phoenix Grace: Faster recovery of Qi and resistance to prolonged debuffs.

Backgrounds (for reference):
- **Reborn Phoenix**, **Flame Phoenix Lineage**, **Ice Phoenix Heritage**, **Wind Phoenix Clan**, **Sun Phoenix Bloodline**, **Moon Phoenix Heritage**, **Eternal Phoenix**

## 6. Celestial (3% — lawful, ordained)
Celestials are embodiments of cosmic law and order. They excel at judgment, healing, and enforcing balance; their presence often shifts moral outcomes and group dynamics.

Suggested passives (3–4):
- Divine Edict: +12% effectiveness versus chaotic or corrupt foes.
- Empyreal Ward: Reduced corruption and increased resistance to dark arts for self and nearby allies.
- Judgement Strike: Conditional bonus damage against those who violate cosmic rules.
- Heaven's Boon: Minor boost to healing received and meditation speed.

Backgrounds (for reference):
- **Heavenly Court Noble**, **Celestial Guardian**, **Heavenly Scholar**, **Fallen Celestial**, **Star Born Celestial**, **Celestial Judge**, **Celestial Healer**

## 7. Asura (1.5% — martial, relentless)
Asura are the personification of conflict: disciplined, fierce, and honed by endless battles. They reward aggression and battlefield mastery.

Suggested passives (3–4):
- Bloodlust Momentum: Stacking damage bonus while continuously engaged in combat.
- Warborn Discipline: Reduced cooldowns or resource costs for martial techniques.
- Tactical Acumen: Passive party coordination bonus when leading a group.
- Iron Will: Strong resistance to fear and charm effects.

Backgrounds (for reference):
- **Asura Warlord**, **Asura Berserker**, **Asura Tactician**, **Exiled Asura**, **Asura Scholar**, **Asura Mercenary**, **Asura Spirit**

## 8. Fox (4% — cunning, charismatic)
Fox spirits are clever, adaptable, and socially skilled. They favor trickery, illusions, and using influence over brute force.

Suggested passives (3–4):
- Trickster's Guile: +15% success chance for stealth, deception, and escape maneuvers.
- Charming Presence: +10% effectiveness in social encounters and reduced suspicion.
- Kitsune Lore: Enhanced potency and reduced cooldowns for illusions and enchantments.
- Tailgift: Occasional small luck bonus that can turn critical moments in the player's favor.

Backgrounds (for reference):
- **Nine-Tailed Fox**, **Mountain Fox**, **City Fox**, **Healing Fox**, **Fox Warrior**, **Fox Scholar**, **Celestial Messenger Fox**

## 9. Qilin (0.5% — auspicious, protective)
Qilin are rare omens of fortune and justice. They excel at protective magics, healing, and detecting imbalance.

Suggested passives (3–4):
- Auspicious Aura: Slight boost to loot quality and reduced chance of critical failure for the party.
- Purifying Step: Automatically remove minor curses when resting or entering a new area.
- Guardian's Blessing: Allies in proximity receive a defensive bonus.
- Righteous Sight: Improved detection of hidden malice or illusions.

Backgrounds (for reference):
- **Royal Qilin**, **Guardian Qilin**, **Judgment Qilin**, **Healing Qilin**, **Storm Qilin**, **Earth Qilin**, **Celestial Qilin**

## 10. Monkey (1.2% — nimble, irreverent)
Monkeys are quick, inventive, and unpredictable. They prefer improvisation, mobility, and creative problem-solving in combat and exploration.

Suggested passives (3–4):
- Agile Evasion: +12% dodge and improved mobility.
- Improvised Tactics: Bonus when using environmental objects or improvised weapons.
- Mimicry: Temporarily copy a non-ultimate technique observed in recent combat.
- Light-Footed Luck: Increased chance to avoid critical failures on quick actions.

Backgrounds (for reference):
- **Monkey King**, **Mountain Monkey**, **Scholar Monkey**, **Trickster Monkey**, **Monkey Warrior**, **Healing Monkey**, **Celestial Messenger Monkey**

## 11. Heavenly (0.8% — privileged, institutional)
Heavenly-born are formal agents of the celestial bureaucracy: structured, influential, and trained in ritual power. They bring authority and ritual support.

Suggested passives (3–4):
- Bureaucratic Authority: Easier access to temple resources and official favors.
- Ritual Mastery: Reduced cost or time for rituals and long-form techniques.
- Protocol Shield: Resistance to social manipulation and legal penalties.
- Envoy's Presence: Improved outcomes when mediating or negotiating between factions.

Backgrounds (for reference):
- **Heavenly Noble**, **Heavenly Warrior**, **Heavenly Scholar**, **Heavenly Messenger**

## 12. Devil (0.8% — cunning, corrosive)
Devils use temptation, bargains, and corruption as tools. They excel at trading short-term gain for hidden long-term advantages and manipulating desires.

Suggested passives (3–4):
- Bargain's Edge: Can trade resources for power with a small hidden cost (corruption or reputation).
- Corrupting Touch: Attacks have a chance to apply a lingering corruption effect.
- Shadow's Guile: +15% advantage on stealth and infiltration in urban/night settings.
- Infernal Contracts: Better rewards when completing morally ambiguous tasks.

Backgrounds (for reference):
- **Devil Prince**, **Devil Temptor**, **Infernal Contractor**, **Shadow Devil**
